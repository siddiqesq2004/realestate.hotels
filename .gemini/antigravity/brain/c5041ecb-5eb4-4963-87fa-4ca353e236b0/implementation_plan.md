# Re-aligning Site for Hotel Real Estate Business

This plan details how we will audit and update the entire site's copy, layout, and dummy pages to perfectly reflect a **B2B / B2C Luxury Hotel Real Estate Business** (where the company develops, manages, and sells branded hotel residences and resorts).

## Proposed Changes

### Global Branding & Navigation
- **Focus**: Shift the messaging from booking a "hotel stay" to "hotel real estate investment and branded residency". 
- **Action**: Update `Navigation.tsx` (if necessary) to ensure the Enquire button specifically targets "Real Estate Enquiries".

### `app/page.tsx` (Homepage)
- Update the introductory overlays in the cinematic scroll to introduce "Aura" as a premier developer of luxury hotel real estate, rather than just a single hotel.
- Ensure the CTA button clearly drives users to explore the real estate portfolio.

### `app/property/page.tsx`
- **Copy**: Change the focus from a single property to highlighting the flagship "Grand Heritage" hotel residence. 
- **Portfolio Section**: Rename "Explore Other Properties" to "Explore Our Hotel Portfolio". Update the dummy property names to sound like hotel real estate (e.g., *Villa Serenity Resort & Residences*, *Azure Hotel Estates*).

### Dummy Property Pages (`app/property/*/page.tsx`)
- Update the 3 dummy pages to explicitly sell hotel real estate, mentioning "hotel amenities", "residence ownership", and "rental yield programs".

### `app/experience/page.tsx`
- **Copy**: Shift the language from "your stay" to "ownership experience" and "branded residency". Highlight that owners get 5-star hotel services year-round.

### `app/investment/page.tsx`
- Ensure the numbers and pitch sound like a high-end real estate developer selling hotel suites and private villas to investors. (This page is already close, but we will tighten the copy).

### `app/location/page.tsx`
- Frame the "Global Destinations" as upcoming hotel real estate developments available for investment.
